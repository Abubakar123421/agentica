# Fictional complaint photos

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for local Escalera testing. These images are
synthetic and are not evidence of an actual food safety incident. They are attached to the seeded
importer demo complaints in the local database. No application code depends on these source files.
Printed dates and decorative barcode stripes are illustrative; the demo fixture maps
`DEMO-CHOC-38` to product `CHOC-100`, manufacturer lot `38`.

## Packaging prompt

Create a realistic fictional customer evidence photograph for a food safety workflow hackathon. Landscape photo on a kitchen counter, a sealed dark forest green chocolate wrapper with cream label, fictional brand SweetCo, product Alpine Milk Chocolate 100g, a close clear rear packaging area with printed LOT 38 and fictional demo barcode caption DEMO-CHOC-38. Include a prominent physical paper card in the scene reading FICTIONAL DEMO IMAGE. No real company branding. Smartphone snapshot with natural light, product and lot legible, not an advertisement. This is synthetic evidence for software testing, not proof of contamination.
